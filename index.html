Nexus

The Everything Medicare operations app for your team.

Download for Windows
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”: click More info, then Run anyway. This shows up for new apps that Windows doesn’t recognize yet.

Install in a minute

  1. Download the installer with the button above.
  2. Open Nexus-Setup.exe from your Downloads. It installs for your Windows account only, so no administrator is needed, and opens Nexus when it’s done.
  3. Enter your access code the first time Nexus opens. The Nexus owner gives you this code, and each computer asks for it only once.
  4. Sign in with the work email and temporary password the Nexus owner gave you, then choose your own password.

Good to know

Updates itself

Nexus installs new versions in the background. You never need to download it again.

Keeps your data

Updating or reinstalling keeps your sign-ins, settings and saved work.

Shortcuts included

Find Nexus on your desktop and in the Start menu after installing.
